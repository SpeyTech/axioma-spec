## 1. Reason

SRS-007-SHALL-043 Option A declares an `evidence_refs` array of 1,024 entries. The `AX:PROOF:v1` canonical record is serialised into a 16,384-byte buffer. A record with more than 192 references can fail to serialise for valid string content. In addition, the producer setters accepted input that let the encoder read outside the record (finding D-11 and its preconditions P1 and P2).

## 2. Amended requirements

**SRS-007-SHALL-043 (Option A table, as amended).** The row "`evidence_refs` array: 1024 entries" is replaced by two rows:

| Structure | Maximum | Declaration |
|---|---|---|
| `AX:PROOF:v1` evidence references, producer | **192** | Compile-time constant `AX_PROOF_MAX_EVIDENCE_REFS`, derived in §3 |
| Merkle closure and compliance structures | 1,024 | Compile-time constant `AX_MAX_EVIDENCE_REFS` (unchanged) |

**SRS-007-AMD-002-SHALL-001: Producer bound.**
- A producer SHALL NOT add more than 192 evidence references to an `AX:PROOF:v1` record.
- The 193rd addition SHALL be rejected with an overflow fault, leaving the record unchanged.
- Every finalisation path SHALL refuse a record holding more than 192 references, however they were written.

**SRS-007-AMD-002-SHALL-002: Verification is not narrowed.**
- A verifier SHALL NOT reject an existing `AX:PROOF:v1` record solely because it holds more than 192 references.
- Verification is bounded only by the serialisation buffer. Valid records produced before this amendment, up to 237 references with short strings, keep their canonical bytes and their verification outcome.

**SRS-007-AMD-002-SHALL-003: Ordering metadata strings (P1).** Each ordering-metadata string (`description`, `key_field`, `direction`) SHALL be terminated within its declared array. Otherwise setting the metadata SHALL be rejected with an overflow fault, leaving the record unchanged.

**SRS-007-AMD-002-SHALL-004: Enumerated values (P2).**
- An out-of-range `result` or `violation` passed to the result setter SHALL raise an integrity fault.
- The fault SHALL be latched in the record, so that every later finalisation of that record fails. The setter's signature is unchanged.
- Producers SHALL also refuse an out-of-range `proof_type` or `evidence_ordering` at finalisation.

**SRS-007-AMD-002-SHALL-005: Encoder bounds.** The canonical encoder SHALL fail with a fault, and SHALL NOT read outside the record, when any of the following holds:
- an enumerated value is out of range;
- the reference count exceeds 1,024;
- a string field is not terminated within its array.

**Verification:** boundary test, negative test, sanitizer test, fingerprint (§4).

## 3. Derivation (normative for the constant)

The canonical `AX:PROOF:v1` record with `k ≥ 1` evidence references is exactly `F + 67·k − 1` bytes, where:
- each reference costs 64 hex characters, 2 quotes and 1 comma, less one comma overall;
- `F` is the size at `k = 0`.

`F` is largest, 3,493 bytes, when all of the following hold:
- `claim` (255), `rule_id` (63) and the three metadata strings (127, 31 and 15) are at maximum length, made of bytes that encode as 6-byte `\u00XX` escapes;
- ordering metadata is present;
- the enum strings are the longest the setters admit;
- `ledger_seq` is 2^64 − 1;
- `proof_hash` is present.

The largest `k` with `3,493 + 67·k − 1 ≤ 16,384` is **192**, which gives 16,356 bytes; 193 gives 16,423 bytes. For the smallest record, `F` is 450 bytes, which allows 237 references.

Two independent derivations agree:
- an analytic model written from the encoder source;
- measurement through the public API.

Both are in the Stage 1 package (`proposals/d11/`). The implementation checks the bound at compile time.

## 5. Compatibility

- **`AX:PROOF:v1` bytes:** no change for any record of 192 references or fewer.
- **Producers:** can no longer create records of 193 to 237 references. Such records were possible before only with short string content.
- **Existing records:** unaffected (SHALL-002).
- **API:** no signature changes.
- **Rejected inputs:** P1 and P2 reject only inputs that previously produced mislabelled content or out-of-bounds reads.

## 6. Out of scope

Finding D-23 is not addressed by this amendment. Every finalised record fails `ax_proof_verify_hash` on `93d75a6`, because `proof_hash` and `commitment` are computed while the `commitment` field is still zero. It needs its own decision, because it concerns what the hash covers.
